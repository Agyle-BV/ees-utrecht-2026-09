How we stay compliant

Secure and compliant, by design

GDPR

  • We are the processor, the entity stays controller. DPA in place.
  • Only the fields the process needs.
  • Pseudonymised where possible.
  • Deleted after the proof of concept, confirmed in writing.

EU AI Act

  • Document tools: minimal risk.
  • Recruitment screening: high risk, built accordingly.
  • A person takes every decision. Every judgement cites its source.
  • Candidates informed, logs kept.

Data residency

  • Data stays in the EU, separated per entity.
  • Models through the API only. Nothing fine-tuned, nothing trained on your data.
  • Zero retention available.
  • Same models inside your Google Cloud tenant, EU region.

Your own IT policy

  • Production inside your tenant: your login, key vault, pipeline, repository.
  • Built to the entity's policy.
  • One EES entity already wrote one for AI delivery. We pass its check.
